# Census Demographics & Vulnerability Indicators - Tamil Nadu

## Dataset Overview
- **Source**: Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India.
- **URL**: [https://censusindia.gov.in/](https://censusindia.gov.in/)
- **Spatial Coverage**: District level across Tamil Nadu state.
- **Indicators**: Total Households, Urban/Rural %, Literacy Rate, Pukka Housing %, Slum Population %, Vulnerability Index.
- **License**: Open Government Data (OGD) License India.

## Acquisition Method
Generated by `src/data_collection/download_census.py` saving output to `datasets/raw/census/census_tamil_nadu.csv`.
